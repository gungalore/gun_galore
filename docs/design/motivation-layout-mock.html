1Application

I, Gerhard Johan Petrus Fourie, identity number 890512 5220 089, apply under section 13 of the Firearms Control Act 60 of 2000 for a licence to possess a Glock handgun in 9mm Parabellum, serial number ZABA01892, for self-defence. (Annexure A: identity document)

I live at 36 Sterappel Crescent, Langeberg Glen, Kraaifontein, in a house, and I work as an electrician. (Annexure D: proof of address)

2Why I need a firearm

I live in the Kraaifontein policing precinct. SAPS's published figures for the station for April to June 2026 record 95 burglaries at residential premises, 241 robberies with aggravating circumstances, 16 robberies at residential premises and 15 carjackings. (Annexure I: station figures)

My work takes me through Dunoon and Elsiesrivier, and I come and go at hours when the street is empty. In the Bothasig precinct, which covers Dunoon, the same quarter recorded 19 aggravated robberies, 3 carjackings and 15 residential burglaries; in Goodwood, which covers Elsiesrivier, 36 aggravated robberies, 7 carjackings and 34 residential burglaries. Local press reported a quadruple murder in Dunoon and a series of snatch-and-grab attacks in Elsiesrivier in the last month. (Annexure I: press clippings)

I also hike on trails away from help, fish at remote spots and travel to and from farms. On those roads I am alone if I stop, and help is not minutes away.

3What I have already done, and why it is not enough

My property is enclosed by a wall with an electric fence, has a guarded entrance, an alarm with armed response, burglar bars and security gates. These protect the house while I am inside it and the power is on. They do nothing on the road, on a trail, at a farm gate, or in my driveway when I arrive home, which is where SAPS's own figures say the attacks happen. A firearm is the one measure that goes with me.

4The firearm, and how I would keep it

A handgun is the only firearm that can be carried on the person and kept out of sight, which a rifle or shotgun cannot. 9mm Parabellum is the common service calibre in South Africa, so ammunition and training are readily available. I would carry the firearm concealed on my person when away from home, keep it at home for the defence of the house, and have it with me in my vehicle. At home it is stored in a rifle safe bolted to the floor, to which only I hold the key. (Annexure E: photographs of the safe)

7Section 13 applied to my case

13(1) A firearm in respect of which a licence may be issued in terms of this section is any (a) shotgun which is not fully or semi-automatic; or (b) handgun which is not fully automatic. (2) The Registrar may issue a licence under this section to any natural person who (a) needs a firearm for self-defence; and (b) cannot reasonably satisfy that need by means other than the possession of a firearm. (3) No person may hold more than one licence issued in terms of this section.

13(1)(b): the Glock is a semi-automatic handgun; it is not fully automatic. 13(2)(a): section 2 above. 13(2)(b): section 3 above. 13(3): I hold no other section 13 licence.
