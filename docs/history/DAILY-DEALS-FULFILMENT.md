**Mission:** Build the sell-first-buy-after half of the OneDayOnly model that DD-1..DD-5 deliberately left out. Today a house-deal sale auto-accepts and immediately tries to courier-book from a non-existent address, with no supplier ever notified. After this build: deal ends/sells out → **Zoho Books Purchase Order** created against the supplier (auto-added as a Zoho **Vendor**) and **emailed to them by Zoho** for exactly the units sold → operator taps **"Stock ready"** → **The Courier Guy** (never Pudo) collects from the **supplier's warehouse** and delivers each buyer's parcel individually.

## 0. Adopted decisions (operator-confirmed defaults — do not re-litigate)

1. **PO trigger = deal ENDED or SOLD_OUT** (never at the first `endsAt` — Extra Time can extend a stocked deal, `deals.service.ts:888-895`). The **killswitch-end path does NOT auto-PO** (call site `deals.service.ts:815` ends deals because the operator turned the programme off — surface a "Place PO" button instead).
2. **Stock-ready = operator button** on the deal cockpit (no auto-timer in v1).
3. **PO → Bill conversion stays manual/accountant** (Zoho POs are non-posting; converting to a Bill when the supplier invoices is an accounting act).
4. **TCG door-to-door ONLY** for deal listings. No Pudo, no collection.
5. **PO emailing is gated** behind a new Setting `deal_po_email_enabled` (default **OFF**) so no real supplier ever gets emailed during development/testing. PO *creation* in Zoho is gated by the existing `ZOHO_BOOKS_ENABLED`.
6. **PO units are pinned at trigger time** using the paid-non-refunded filter (`deals.service.ts:444-455` — same filter as P&L/cap). Refunds landing after the PO is cut are handled operationally (admin sees both numbers); do NOT build an automatic PO-amendment engine in v1.
